## 1. Who We Are

LemonGuard ("**we**", "**us**", or "**LemonGuard**") is a used-car
self-inspection app provided by **Xiaoling Ling**, an individual developer. This
Privacy Policy explains what information the LemonGuard app and the website at
lemonguard.app (together, the "**Service**") collect, how it is used, and the
choices you have.

By using the Service, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree,
please do not use the Service.

## 2. Summary — The Short Version

LemonGuard is built to be **local-first and privacy-respecting**:

- **No account required.** You can use the Service without signing up or giving
  us your name or email.
- **Your inspection data stays on your device.** Inspections, checklist
  answers, notes, and photos are stored locally in the app on your device. We
  do not upload them to our servers, and we cannot see them.
- **We do not run analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs** in the app.
- The only personal data that leaves your device is what is necessary to
  **process in-app purchases**, which is handled by Apple (or Google) and our
  payments provider, RevenueCat — not by us directly.

The sections below explain this in detail.

## 3. Information We Do Not Collect

We do **not** collect, store, or have access to:

- your name, email address, phone number, or postal address (unless you
  voluntarily email us — see Section 7);
- your inspection records, checklist answers, notes, or photos;
- your precise location, contacts, or browsing history;
- analytics, usage tracking, advertising identifiers, or behavioral profiles.

## 4. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

The following data is created and stored **only on your device**, within the
app's storage. We do not receive or have access to it:

- **Inspection data** — the vehicles you add, checklist answers (Pass / Flag /
  Fail / Skip), scores, grades, and repair-cost estimates.
- **Notes** — any text you write about an item or vehicle.
- **Photos** — images you capture or attach as inspection evidence. These are
  stored in the app's storage on your device.
- **App preferences** — such as your light/dark theme choice.

This data remains on your device until you delete it within the app or delete
the app itself. It may also be included in any device backup you create (for
example, an iCloud or Google backup); those backups are controlled by you and
your device platform, not by us.

## 5. Camera, Microphone, and Photo Library Permissions

The app may ask for these device permissions so it can function:

- **Camera** — to let you photograph vehicle issues as inspection evidence.
- **Photo Library** — to let you attach existing photos to an inspection.
- **Microphone** — required by the device for video capture.

These permissions are used **only** for the features you trigger. Photos and any
media you capture are stored locally on your device (see Section 4). You can
grant or revoke these permissions at any time in your device settings; some
features will not work without them.

## 6. In-App Purchases

LemonGuard offers an optional one-time in-app purchase. Purchases are processed
by the **Apple App Store** (or **Google Play Store**) and by our payments
provider, **RevenueCat, Inc.**, which we use to validate purchases and unlock
the app.

- We do **not** collect or store your payment-card or billing details. Those
  are handled by Apple or Google.
- To validate and restore purchases, RevenueCat and the app store process a
  purchase receipt and a pseudonymous app-user identifier and store-assigned
  identifiers. This allows the app to confirm whether you have purchased it.
- RevenueCat acts as our service provider for this purpose. See RevenueCat's
  privacy policy at https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy and Apple's or Google's
  privacy policies for how they handle purchase information.

## 7. If You Contact Us

If you email us (for example, to send feedback or ask for support), we will
receive your email address and whatever information you choose to include. We
use it only to respond to you and to provide support. We do not use it for
marketing.

## 8. How We Use Information

We use the limited information described above only to:

- operate, maintain, and provide the Service and its features;
- process and validate in-app purchases and restore them on request;
- respond to your support requests; and
- comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service.

We do **not** sell or rent personal information, and we do not use it for
targeted advertising.

## 9. Sharing of Information

We do not share personal information with third parties except:

- **Service providers** — Apple, Google, and RevenueCat, solely to process
  in-app purchases as described in Section 6.
- **Legal reasons** — if required by law, legal process, or to protect the
  rights, safety, or property of LemonGuard or others.
- **Business transfer** — if the Service is involved in a merger, acquisition,
  or sale of assets, information may be transferred as part of that
  transaction, subject to this Privacy Policy.

## 10. Data Retention and Deletion

Because inspection data, notes, and photos are stored only on your device, you
control them directly. To delete this data, delete the relevant inspections
within the app, or uninstall the app to remove all of it.

Purchase records held by Apple, Google, or RevenueCat are retained under those
companies' own policies.

## 11. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required
in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from
children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, please
contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

## 12. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights regarding your personal
information — such as the right to access, correct, or delete it, or to object
to certain processing.

- Because we do not collect or store your inspection data or contact details on
  our servers, most of your data is already under your direct control on your
  device.
- For purchase-related data, contact Apple, Google, or RevenueCat.
- For any request relating to information we do hold (such as an email you sent
  us), contact us using Section 14. We will respond as required by applicable
  law. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

**California residents:** we do not "sell" or "share" personal information as
those terms are defined under the CCPA/CPRA.

## 13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material
changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate,
provide notice within the app or on the website. Your continued use of the
Service after the changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated
policy.
